Moon textures
=============

Both files come from NASA's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720),
built from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. NASA imagery is public domain:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

- `lunar-surface.webp` — 8192 × 4096, WebP quality 92

Encoded from `lroc_color_poles_8k.tif`, the natural-color LROC wide-angle
camera mosaic (Hapke normalized, gamma corrected, and white balanced in the
CGI Moon Kit). The map is centered on 0° longitude, which matches the
landing-site coordinates.

- `lunar-bumpmap.webp` — 5760 × 2880, WebP quality 100

Encoded from `ldem_16_uint.tif`, the LOLA global elevation grid at 16 pixels
per degree. The 16-bit half-meter elevations were contrast-stretched to 8-bit
grayscale for use as a bump map. The native width is already under 8192, so
it is not upscaled.

The landing-site list began with the globe.gl example. Chang'e 5 and Chang'e 6
were added from published landing coordinates:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0012821X2100114X
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-59443-5
