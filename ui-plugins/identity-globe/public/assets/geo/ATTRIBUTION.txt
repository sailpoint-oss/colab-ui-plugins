# Gazetteer attribution

`cities.json` is derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/)
(`cities15000`, `admin1CodesASCII`, and `countryInfo`), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The included places are filtered, and the names and coordinates are normalized.
The raw GeoNames dumps are not included.
